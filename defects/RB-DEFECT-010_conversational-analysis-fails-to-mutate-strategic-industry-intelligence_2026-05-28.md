# RB-DEFECT-010 — Conversational Analysis Fails To Mutate Strategic Industry Intelligence

**Date opened:** 2026-05-28  
**Severity:** High  
**Priority:** High  
**Category:** Relationship Intelligence Persistence / Industry Intelligence Mutation / CoS Operationalization  
**Status:** Resolved — RB 9.53 (2026-06-04)  
**Sprint:** RB 9.20R — reality reconciliation, dirty-tree cleanup, and mutation-proof hardening

## Summary

RB successfully analyzed and synthesized a strategic discussion regarding Toast's
"Regulars Report 2026" and the broader implications for restaurant technology
customer retention, but failed to operationalize the intelligence into
persistent ecosystem structures.

The system behaved as a highly capable conversational advisor rather than a
continuously mutating Chief of Staff intelligence platform.

Expected RB behavior would have included:

- automatic extraction of durable industry intelligence
- mutation of the restaurant industry graph
- mutation of Todd's strategic positioning profile
- persistence of reusable thought-leadership themes
- classification of strategic market signals
- tagging of future-use content opportunities
- retrieval readiness for future daily briefs, strategy sessions, and
  relationship-intelligence queries

## Observed Behavior

RB generated strong conversational analysis and LinkedIn drafting assistance but
did not demonstrate evidence that the information was:

- persisted
- structured
- graph-mutated
- relationship-linked
- surfaced as reusable intelligence
- integrated into future strategic reasoning context

## Conversation Context

Discussion centered around Toast's newsletter and "Regulars Report 2026," which
highlighted:

- 7% of guests driving up to 50% of order volume
- importance of retention and loyalty economics
- operational predictability from regular guests
- reasons customers stop returning
- operational erosion versus catastrophic failures

The user then introduced a strategic interpretation:

> Restaurant tech companies need to take a note from restaurant customers and
> value retention over new customer acquisition as well.

The conversation evolved into a broader operational thesis:

- restaurant tech vendors over-index on acquisition
- many vendors rely on operational stickiness instead of relationship trust
- customer churn often begins through gradual erosion
- retention in restaurant tech mirrors hospitality principles
- "hard to replace" is not the same as a valued relationship

## Acceptance Criteria

A successful fix must:

1. Detect durable strategic insights inside conversational analysis.
2. Classify insight type: industry trend, vendor positioning, user positioning,
   thought-leadership theme, market signal, relationship implication, or action
   opportunity.
3. Emit review-first mutation proposals for industry graph and user strategic
   positioning memory.
4. Persist confirmed strategic insights to durable memory with source,
   timestamp, confidence, claim status, and future-use tags.
5. Link retention/trust/customer-success insights to existing restaurant-tech
   realism and operational AI realism themes when evidence supports it.
6. Make the insight retrievable through strategic-memory queries and eligible
   for future Daily Brief synthesis.
7. Preserve guardrails: no unsupported factual claims, no unverified vendor
   graph promotion, no invented relationship history, and no silent high-impact
   mutation without review.
8. Add regression tests proving the Toast / Regulars Report scenario produces
   structured mutation proposals and future retrieval readiness.
