## Detailed Discussion

### Introduction and Background
Monica Grinkis introduced herself and outlined her role and experience with IKEA, emphasizing her focus on payments. She explained the need for a creative POS solution for a food truck, which is part of a broader initiative to expand IKEA's food offerings beyond traditional settings.

### Current Solutions and Challenges
Monica detailed the current use of Fiserv's Clover solution for small-scale operations, noting its limitations for the food truck due to space constraints and the need for a more integrated solution. She mentioned the "store in a box" solution but highlighted its impracticality for the mobile setup of a food truck.

### Requirements for the New POS System
Monica expressed the need for a POS system that could:
- Operate on a tablet with limited inventory.
- Handle multiple tax jurisdictions automatically.
- Include a kitchen display system (KDS) for order tracking.
- Be ADA compliant, allowing easy access for all customers.
- Possibly support line busting and QR code-based ordering.

### Proposed Solutions
Michael Schwartz and Ray Morily presented various POS solutions, including:
- A handheld device similar to a cell phone size.
- A tablet-based system with cellular capabilities.
- Modular systems that could be customized for different configurations.
- The potential for integrating with IKEA's existing systems in the future.

### ADA Compliance and Payment Options
Monica raised concerns about ADA compliance, particularly regarding the accessibility of payment terminals for customers in wheelchairs. The discussion also confirmed that the food truck would only accept credit payments, eliminating the need for cash handling.

### Future Considerations
The conversation touched on potential future integrations with IKEA's systems and the possibility of expanding the food truck initiative if successful. There was also a brief discussion on the potential for QR code-based ordering and payment.
