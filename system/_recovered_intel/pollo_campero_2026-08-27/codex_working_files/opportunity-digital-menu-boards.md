# Pollo Campero USA - Digital Menu Boards Opportunity

## Opportunity thesis

Win the requested DMB problem first: unify content management, improve accuracy and agility, modernize indoor and drive-thru displays, integrate with the existing POS environment, reuse viable assets, and assume accountable lifecycle support. Position broader Genius + Worldpay consolidation as an optional roadmap rather than a prerequisite.

## Required scope

- Project kickoff, governance and project management.
- Site surveys for existing and new restaurants.
- Compatibility assessment and reuse of viable existing assets.
- Evaluation of customer-owned Stream inventory held at the warehouse.

- Outdoor DMB configuration support.

## Optional scope

- In-store music.
- Guest Wi-Fi.

## RFI commitments requiring protection or validation

1. Native pricing and order-confirmation integrations were claimed for Genius POS, PAR Brink and NCR.
2. Oracle was positioned as an API-enabled third-party integration requiring agreements and potentially fees.
3. Near-real-time pricing and availability were represented as supported, with timing dependent on integration and display ingestion settings.
4. Dynamic upselling was described as supported, but full capability was qualified as native to the Genius ecosystem and dependent on third-party POS APIs elsewhere.
5. Existing displays were represented as generally reusable after model, age, condition and compatibility assessment.
6. Controller and controllerless deployment models were offered, including a dual-display media-player architecture.
7. Installation, testing and go-live/event support costs were stated as included in the proposal, although the RFI contained no final pricing.
8. Example response and resolution SLAs were supplied and could be interpreted as commitments.
9. Three-year warranty coverage was stated for all hardware/devices included in pricing.
10. ADA, local-code and environmental compliance was stated broadly and must be narrowed to the final engineered and installed scope.

## Bid decision

**Proceed with controlled qualifications.** The solution is directionally strong, but unqualified compliance would expose Global Payments to integration, logistics, warranty, SLA and margin risk.

## Commercial structure

- One-time discovery and project kickoff.
- Per-location site survey.
- Per-device compatibility/inventory assessment where applicable.
- Per-device/configuration hardware pricing.
- One-time integration development/certification plus recurring support where applicable.
- Per-location/lane order-confirmation pricing.
- Recurring CMS licensing per screen or location.
- Recurring managed-content services with included request volumes.
- Per-configuration/location installation.
- Per-location/device de-installation.
- Monthly storage per device, pallet or occupied footprint.
- Per-device testing, refurbishment and redeployment preparation.
- Freight at actual or defined schedule.
- Recurring support by SLA tier.
- Optional extended warranty.
