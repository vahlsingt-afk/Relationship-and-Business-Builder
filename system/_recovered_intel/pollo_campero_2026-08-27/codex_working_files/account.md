# Pollo Campero USA

## Executive intelligence

Pollo Campero USA is conducting a formal competitive procurement to replace its fragmented and aging digital menu board environment with a unified, scalable indoor and drive-thru platform. The opportunity is an immediate DMB engagement and a potential land-and-expand relationship for Global Payments/Genius and Worldpay; it is not presently a forced POS-displacement motion.

## Verified operating context

- Approximately 150 U.S. locations are stated in the RFP.
- Current DMB infrastructure uses multiple vendors, including ZoneIn and Stream, and relies on highly manual processes.
- Typical indoor configuration is five displays: four 42-inch landscape and one 47-inch portrait.
- Indoor equipment is a mix of Samsung and LG.
- Typical outdoor configuration uses 55-inch LG displays: three or four screens depending on presell configuration and six at dual-lane locations.
- Most drive-thru locations have a canopy and presell board, but configurations vary.
- Order confirmation is displayed directly on the outdoor menu screen and remains mandatory.
- Speakers and microphones are outside the RFP scope.
- The required integration environment includes Oracle and NCR POS platforms.

## Business objectives

- Modernize indoor and outdoor menu boards.
- Centralize content, pricing, promotions, dayparting and scheduling.
- Automate price and item-availability updates.
- Preserve brand consistency while allowing governed field/franchise flexibility.
- Reduce manual work for IT and Marketing.
- Support new-store growth and future integrations.
- Enable order confirmation and potential transaction-aware dynamic upselling.
- Establish installation, de-installation, asset-management, support and warranty capabilities.
- Evaluate optional music and guest Wi-Fi services for vendor consolidation.

## Technology and relationship context

- Known incumbent restaurant stack context includes NCR/Aloha, NCR back office/network services, Olo and Punchh; exact current versions and location distribution require validation.
- Global Payments submitted the initial Genius DMB RFI response.
- Worldpay/Global Payments scale and potential vendor-consolidation economics are strategically relevant but should support, not distract from, the DMB response.

## Current posture

Proceed with a controlled, qualified bid. Lead with DMB lifecycle accountability and reuse economics. Do not make unqualified commitments for Oracle integration, third-party transaction-aware upselling, reused-equipment warranty, local-code compliance, or asset storage until internal owners approve the operating model.
