## Customer clarification questions

1. Provide the location-level inventory of displays, players/controllers, mounts, lane configurations, age and ownership.
2. Identify the Oracle and NCR products and versions deployed by location.
3. Identify the authoritative sources for menu, price, availability and inventory data.
4. Define measurable “near-real-time” latency and availability expectations.
5. Define the required order-confirmation fields and lane-to-order mapping.
6. Confirm whether transaction-aware upselling is mandatory at launch or acceptable as a pilot/later phase.
7. Provide the detailed Stream warehouse inventory and permit inspection before binding pricing.
8. Define pilot size, rollout waves, completion target and annual new-store volume.
9. Define standard, urgent and emergency content-request volumes and turnaround expectations.
10. Define required support hours, severity levels, restoration targets and onsite dispatch expectations.
11. Confirm whether unit pricing and scenario totals are acceptable pending site validation.
12. Identify responsibility for permits, structural work, electrical remediation, networking and civil work.

## Submission gate

Do not certify the SOW pricing sheet until every required row has:

- A compliance status;
- Deliverable description;
- Assumptions and dependencies;
- Exclusions;
- Billing basis;
- Approved fee or an explicitly approved pending-price treatment;
- Internal owner approval.
