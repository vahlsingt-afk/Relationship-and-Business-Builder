## Principal response corrections

- Do not use a blanket “Yes” where third-party access or discovery determines compliance.
- Distinguish proven production integrations from API extensibility.
- Separate scheduled/rules-based suggestive selling from live transaction-aware upselling.
- Replace blanket compliance statements with scoped engineering commitments.
- Reconcile “installation included” with the final unit-price schedule and exclusions.
- Treat example SLAs as nonbinding until Support approves the final service schedule.
- Confirm every warranty claim against the proposed bill of materials.
- Add the entire removed-asset lifecycle as a separately governed and priced service.
