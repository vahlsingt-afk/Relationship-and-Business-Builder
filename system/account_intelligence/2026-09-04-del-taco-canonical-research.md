## Account snapshot

- Del Taco was founded in 1964 and is headquartered in Lake Forest, California.
- Yadav Enterprises acquired Del Taco from Jack in the Box in late 2025. The definitive agreement announced a $115 million cash purchase price; Jack in the Box later reported $119 million after customary adjustments and a December 2025 close.
- At fiscal year-end September 28, 2025, Del Taco had 576 restaurants: 132 company-operated and 444 franchised (77% franchised). Del Taco's current location finder lists 556 U.S. locations, so the live footprint should be reconciled before external use.
- Del Taco reported FY2025 systemwide sales of $918.8 million, down from $956.8 million in 2024. FY2025 system same-store sales declined 3.7%. The brand opened 14 restaurants and closed 32, a net decline of 18 units. Reported FY2025 restaurant-level margin was 11.1% and franchise-level margin was 26.7%.
- FY2025 company-store transactions declined 6.9% while average check increased 4.5%, including approximately 5.5% menu pricing. Company restaurant sales declined 25.3%, driven primarily by fewer average restaurants and weaker transactions.
- Yadav describes Del Taco as part of a portfolio spanning more than 300 franchise restaurants and says it is the largest franchisee of Jack in the Box, Denny's, and TGI Fridays; it is also the franchisor of Taco Cabana and Nick the Greek.

## Ownership and strategic context

The ownership transition is the central account event. Yadav says the acquisition supports long-term growth and a new phase of brand evolution, while preserving Del Taco's legacy and franchisee relationships. In March 2026, Del Taco introduced an 18-month turnaround program called **Project Sunrise**. The plan returns legacy product names, recipes, ingredients, and preparation methods in response to negative guest feedback, while emphasizing consistency, quality, value, simplification, and renewed innovation. In May 2026, the brand added an 11-item value menu with entry pricing starting at $1. This creates a plausible window for operating-model, technology, payments, hardware, content, and vendor-stack reassessment, but no public source reviewed confirms a Del Taco POS replacement decision after the acquisition.

The inherited business entered the transition under pressure. FY2025 systemwide sales and comparable sales were down, restaurant count contracted, and company restaurant margin was modest. Those facts make execution reliability, franchisee economics, rollout cost, hardware lifecycle, and measurable ROI more credible discovery themes than a broad platform-replacement pitch.

## Technology environment

### Publicly confirmed or source-backed

- **POS / unified commerce — Qu (announced 2024):** Qu announced that Jack in the Box would modernize Jack in the Box and Del Taco restaurants on Qu's unified commerce platform across cashier, drive-thru, kiosk, kitchen, online ordering, and fulfillment. Because Yadav acquired Del Taco afterward, current rollout status, contractual inheritance, deployed-store count, and Yadav's go-forward decision require validation.
- **Legacy POS / back office — Radiant/NCR Aloha and MenuLink (historical):** Del Taco selected Radiant in 2006 for Aloha QuickService POS, centralized data management, Radiant terminals, and MenuLink back office. This is historical evidence, not proof of the current production stack.
- **Drive-thru voice AI — Presto (historical/discontinued):** Presto announced an expanded Del Taco deployment in 2022, but a November 2023 securities filing later disclosed that Del Taco decided not to move forward. Do not represent Presto as a current platform without new evidence.
- **Digital ordering / loyalty — Olo:** RB already carries an Olo loyalty relationship at medium confidence. Del Taco's current public site confirms app and online-ordering availability but does not, by itself, establish the underlying vendor or product modules. Validate whether Olo Ordering, Rails, Dispatch, Loyalty, or other modules are currently contracted.
- **Payments — Worldpay:** RB carries Worldpay as confirmed at high confidence. Internal chat evidence says Worldpay was in the middle of a Del Taco contract renewal and that the timing is favorable for Genius engagement. Contract scope, term, renewal date, channels, gateway, acquiring ownership, hardware estate, and commercial decision-makers remain to be validated.
