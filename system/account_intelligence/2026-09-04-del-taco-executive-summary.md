# Del Taco — Executive Summary

Del Taco is now a Yadav Enterprises-owned, predominantly franchised Mexican QSR executing an 18-month post-acquisition turnaround called **Project Sunrise**. Yadav acquired the brand from Jack in the Box in late 2025; the announced purchase price was $115 million, and Jack in the Box later reported $119 million after customary adjustments. At fiscal year-end September 28, 2025, Del Taco had 576 restaurants—132 company-operated and 444 franchised—and generated $918.8 million in systemwide sales. That inherited baseline was under pressure: FY2025 system same-store sales declined 3.7%, company-store transactions declined 6.9% despite about 5.5% menu pricing, 14 restaurants opened, 32 closed, and reported restaurant-level margin was 11.1%. Company restaurant sales fell 25.3%, driven primarily by a lower average restaurant count and weaker transactions.

Yadav's January 2026 leadership reset signals an active transformation agenda. Confirmed leaders include Anil Yadav, CEO of Yadav Enterprises; Ulyses Camacho, Chief Transformation Officer supporting the Del Taco acquisition while continuing as Taco Cabana president and COO; Noah Chillingworth, Del Taco Chief Marketing Officer; and Ellen Sasada, Vice President of Supply Chain, Food Safety, and Quality Assurance. Camacho's mandate includes unit-level execution, cost optimization, and modernization of systems and processes. Chillingworth owns brand strategy, menu and promotional marketing, digital and loyalty platforms, and guest insight. Project Sunrise is returning legacy products and preparation methods, simplifying where needed, and rebuilding around consistency, quality, value, traffic, and guest trust. A May 2026 value-menu launch with 11 items starting at $1 reinforces value as a central traffic lever.
