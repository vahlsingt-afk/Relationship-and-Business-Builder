# McDonald's / Global Payments + Genius Strategic Account Plan

**Date:** 2026-08-06  
**Owner:** Todd Vahlsing  
**Account:** McDonald's Corporation  
**Offer:** Global Payments unified commerce capabilities powered by the Genius restaurant technology portfolio  
**Relationship thesis:** Global Payments/Genius is not unknown to McDonald's because of CosMc's, but the realistic McDonald's opportunity is not to re-sell the CosMc's stack. The three most credible approval paths are second-source drive-thru DMB, POS hardware optionality, and a camera-based drive-thru timer that was used in the CosMc's stack and could provide an alternative to the loop-detector systems used across much of the McDonald's estate. Each path must respect McDonald's existing U.S. platform, global roadmap, and franchise operating reality.  
**Evidence boundary:** CosMc's publicly establishes competitive selection, single-supplier trust, production credibility, broad solution scope, and McDonald's ecosystem experience. It does not, by itself, establish global-scale readiness for the core McDonald's estate.

## 1. Account objective

Secure a McDonald's-sponsored evaluation of one realistic Global Payments/Genius wedge in the core restaurant environment, with DMB second-source optionality as the lead path, POS hardware optionality as the second path, and the CosMc's-proven camera-based drive-thru timer as a third approval path. Use measured operational results to earn approved-solution status without forcing a core-platform replacement conversation.

### Target progression

1. Convert the public CosMc's selection story and internal delivery record into a credibility proof package, not a direct product-scope pitch.
2. Obtain an internal McDonald's coach and executive/operational sponsor.
3. Validate whether DMB second-source need, POS hardware dissatisfaction, or camera-timer modernization is active, funded, and owned.
4. Complete architecture, security, integration, and supplier qualification.
5. Run a tightly scoped validation with pre-agreed success and conversion gates.
6. Earn a U.S. approved-solution or second-source path.
7. Only then evaluate multi-market or global approved-solution relevance.

## 2. Executive positioning

## 8. Initial pilot hypothesis

### Proposed pilot

A focused validation of one of three approval paths in the core estate: second-source DMB, POS hardware optionality, or camera-based drive-thru timing. Genius would integrate with the existing McDonald's environment rather than replace NEWPOS or force a broad platform decision.

### Candidate scope

- Validate Genius-powered DMB performance against McDonald's U.S. drive-thru requirements.
- Demonstrate DMB operation without a separate media player where architecture permits.
- Prove menu, price, promotion, and availability synchronization in the existing McDonald's ecosystem.
- For the timer path, run camera and loop detection side by side to compare arrival/departure capture, lane attribution, timing accuracy, missed/false detections, uptime, and operating consistency across day/night and weather conditions.
- Validate privacy, cybersecurity, image/data retention, network bandwidth, camera placement, installation, calibration, maintenance, and field-support requirements.
- Validate field support, monitoring, remote recovery, and hardware replacement process.
- Test selected POS hardware peripherals or terminal options if McDonald's confirms dissatisfaction with a domestic provider.
- Document integration boundaries with NEWPOS, Edge/server infrastructure, approved hardware standards, and existing DMB workflows.
- Require minimal restaurant disruption and no change to the crew operating model.

### Illustrative success gates

- DMB content accuracy, availability, and recovery meet or exceed incumbent standards.
- Architecture reduces device/media-player complexity or support burden.
- No negative impact on NEWPOS, Edge/server infrastructure, or restaurant operations.
- Hardware reliability, swap process, and support model satisfy McDonald's field expectations.
- If the timer path is selected, camera timing meets pre-agreed accuracy and availability thresholds and demonstrates an installation, maintenance, analytics, or total-cost advantage over the loop-detector baseline.
- McDonald's defines whether the path is second-source, replacement, domestic-only, global-approved, or specialty use-case.
- Named DMB/hardware decision owner and pre-negotiated expansion path before deployment.

## 11. Champion and coach development plan

### Coach qualification questions

A real coach should be willing to provide at least three of the following:

- Explain how the decision will actually be made.
- Identify the real owner of the problem and budget.
- Name likely blockers and incumbent sensitivities.
- Review the message before it is delivered internally.
- Make or sponsor an introduction.
- Share what proof is missing.
- Advise when not to advance the opportunity.

### Champion qualification questions

A real champion should:

- Own or materially benefit from the business outcome.
- Have influence with technology, operations, and/or procurement.
- Invest political capital in the evaluation.
- Help secure restaurants, data, or resources for validation.
- Advocate for Genius when Genius is not in the room.
- Have a credible path to funding or production adoption.

No one in the current baseline should yet be labeled a confirmed McDonald's champion. Bruce is the strongest coach candidate; Amanda, Mike Skog, Brian Gomez, Todd Langeland, or an operations leader could develop into a champion after discovery establishes problem ownership.
