Reported support/service frustration is a credible displacement trigger that should be validated with the account team and customer stakeholders.

The confirmed working footprint is PAR POS + PAR Pay + PAR hardware. Treat other PAR products, contract timing, store coverage, and systemwide standardization as unknown until sourced.

Current leadership correction: remove Richard Heyman from active Scooter's stakeholder maps. Tyler Marpes is the current technology lead; his formal title remains recorded as Vice President, Technology Operations unless newer title evidence appears.
