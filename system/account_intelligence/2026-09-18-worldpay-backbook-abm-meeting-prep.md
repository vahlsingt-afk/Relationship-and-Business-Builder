## Campaign Architecture

### Campaign A — “What's the crack in the stack?”

RM-sponsored 30-minute restaurant technology-stack assessment. Identify one unnecessary cost, integration burden, operational bottleneck, or approaching lifecycle event. CTA: map the current stack—not book a demo.

### Campaign B — “Modernize without ripping everything out”

For wedge accounts with a recent or protected POS. Lead with DMB, drive-thru, hardware, deployment, and integration improvements that preserve the customer's chosen core platform.

### Campaign C — “Fund modernization through the relationship you already have”

For a tightly qualified subset only. Explore whether payment economics and transaction-metered software can improve cash flow and fund modernization. Keep internal until finance, legal, product, billing, and leadership approve the construct.

### Campaign D — “Protect control of the payment and data path”

For accounts exposed to Toast, PAR Pay, Qu Pay, gateway taxes, or payfac disintermediation. Position Genius and Worldpay together around control, transparency, interoperability, and fewer economic leaks.

## Proposed 90-Day Pilot

1. Select **10–15 accounts**, not the entire backbook.
2. Require an identified Worldpay RM and explicit RM sponsorship for every activated account.
3. Complete a one-page ABM card: relationship strength, customer stakeholders, current stack, catalyst, incumbent vulnerability, primary wedge, proof point, and next action.
4. Assign each account **one primary campaign** and one measurable next commitment.
5. Use the RM introduction as the principal activation channel; marketing supports personalization and proof.
6. Measure RM participation, introductions, discovery meetings, verified intelligence, qualified wedges, and pipeline—not clicks or MQLs.
7. Re-score accounts after every material interaction.

## ABM Eligibility Gate

An account enters the backbook ABM cohort only when all of these are true:

1. No active Genius opportunity, RFP, proposal or established customer-specific campaign already owns the motion.
2. No customer-imposed engagement restriction or internal account-ownership conflict exists.
3. No dealer, reseller or channel protection blocks direct activation.
4. The Worldpay RM explicitly sponsors the account and outreach path.
5. RB has enough current stack and relationship intelligence to identify a credible problem hypothesis.
6. The proposed campaign complements rather than duplicates current sales activity.

Commercial importance and a high MAP score do not establish ABM eligibility. They determine account attention only after these exclusion gates are applied.
