# Strategic Operator Rubric

Purpose: define categorical, observable scoring for RB's Strategic Operator Intelligence layer.

RB stores categorical buckets first. Numeric ranking can be derived later, but primary state should remain explainable until there is ground truth.

Levels: `low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`.

## Influence

- `low`: Local or single-market operator with limited visible peer influence.
- `medium`: Multi-unit operator with regional scale, recognizable brand portfolio, or visible local franchisee influence.
- `high`: Large multi-brand operator, major franchisee group, PE-backed platform, or operator whose decisions are likely noticed by peers/vendors.
- `critical`: Category-shaping operator, system-largest franchisee, consolidator, brand-owned strategic vehicle, or operator whose decisions can reset vendor expectations across the ecosystem.

## Relationship Value

- `low`: No known network overlap and no obvious connection to Todd's active threads, target circles, or strategic positioning.
- `medium`: Plausible relevance to Todd's restaurant-tech network, but relationship path is not yet known.
- `high`: Direct or one-hop relationship path, active-thread tie, target-account relevance, or useful conversation hook for known RC/LKI/LMI contacts.
- `critical`: Direct high-trust path or active opportunity where operator intelligence changes today's recommended action.

## Operational Pressure

- `low`: No visible change in scale, complexity, labor burden, reporting needs, or integration pressure.
- `medium`: Some expansion, vendor activity, labor pressure, or operating-model complexity that may affect technology priorities.
- `high`: Acquisition, multi-brand expansion, leadership change, restructuring, vendor transition, integration, or unit growth likely to create standardization/reporting/labor pressure.
- `critical`: Bankruptcy, major restructuring, large acquisition/divestiture, rapid consolidation, or operational crisis likely to force immediate technology, labor, reporting, or vendor decisions.

## Ecosystem Impact

- `low`: Event is interesting but unlikely to influence peers, vendors, franchisors, or investor/operator narratives.
- `medium`: Event signals a trend worth monitoring in one brand, geography, or technology category.
- `high`: Event can influence peer operator buying behavior, vendor positioning, franchisor standards, or regional operating norms.
- `critical`: Event likely changes the broader restaurant-tech/vendor conversation or creates a new reference case for operators, PE groups, or franchisors.

## Future Opportunity

- `low`: No foreseeable buying window, relationship path, or strategic use for Todd.
- `medium`: Monitor for possible relationship path, content angle, or future operator conversation.
- `high`: Likely medium-term buying window, relationship-building target, consulting angle, or active-thread input.
- `critical`: Near-term relationship/action opportunity tied to Todd's active work, warm path, or immediate strategic decision.

## Follow-Up Priority

- `low`: Ignore unless it repeats or maps to a named relationship later.
- `medium`: Monitor; add to watchlist history and revisit if another movement appears.
- `high`: Map relationship paths, watch leadership/vendor/hiring signals, and consider a soft-touch conversation hook this week.
- `critical`: Act today; create a loop, draft an outreach/research action, or use the signal in an active opportunity conversation.

## Proximity Categories

- `direct`: At least one known executive/contact on the operator entity maps to an RC/LKI/LMI in baseline.
- `one_hop`: No direct executive match, but an RC/LKI/LMI works at a portfolio company/brand or has explicit company overlap.
- `two_hop`: No direct match, but an active thread, target circle, or known strategic memory creates a plausible relationship path.
- `none`: No known relationship path after matching.
- `unknown`: Not enough data to resolve; ask a reconciliation question when the operator is strategically material.
