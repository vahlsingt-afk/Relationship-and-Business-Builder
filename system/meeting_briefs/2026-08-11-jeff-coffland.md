**Relationship:** Trusted OTP3 peer since 2010/2011; longtime career supporter; Senior Supplier Manager at McDonald's  
**Tone:** Personal, curious, candid, non-transactional

Leave with the relationship genuinely renewed and a sharper, reality-based understanding of what is changing inside McDonald's and across its technology-vendor ecosystem. A commercial next step is optional; trust and current-state learning are the required outcomes.

## Main objectives

1. **Reestablish the connection.** Reconnect with someone Todd respects in the space, hear what is happening in Jeff's world, and restore an easy peer cadence.
2. **Understand McDonald's today.** Learn where McDonald's is putting attention, where execution or supplier pressure is showing up, and what has materially changed.

4. **Map the correct contacts and decision path.** Understand who owns the McDonald's use cases connected to capabilities Genius demonstrated at CosMc's, who can validate the prior work, and which contacts should be approached first.
5. **Pressure-test carefully.** If the conversation supports it, test whether second-source DMB, hardware optionality, or drive-thru timing are real needs—without pitching Jeff.

## Recommended conversation flow

### 1. Start with Jeff, not the account

- “It has been too long. I wanted to reconnect because I have always respected how you see the industry and supplier side.”
- “I still think about us sitting next to each other in that OTP3 class and how we hit it off immediately.”
- “You were always supportive of me, especially when I made the jump from McDonald's to PAR, and I have never forgotten that.”
- “What has changed most in your world over the last year?”
- “What are you spending more time on now than you expected?”

Share a concise update on the Global Payments/Genius role and what Todd is learning. Keep this short enough that Jeff has room to talk.

### 2. Move into McDonald's operating reality

- What are the biggest technology or execution priorities inside McDonald's right now?
- Where is the organization feeling the most supplier or rollout friction?
- Has the balance between corporate standards, global architecture, U.S. execution, and franchisee adoption changed?
- Which issues are receiving genuine leadership attention versus routine vendor noise?

### 3. Understand the vendor map

- Which technology partners are performing well and earning more trust?
- Where are service, rollout, support, cost, or concentration concerns emerging?
- Has McDonald's appetite for second-source suppliers changed?
- What is the current reality around DMB providers and U.S. execution?
- How does McDonald's currently view POS hardware suppliers and hardware optionality?
- Are drive-thru timing, cameras, or speed-of-service tools being reconsidered?
- Which suppliers or categories should Todd understand better before forming a point of view?

### 4. Pressure-test the Genius thesis only if invited

Use this framing:

> “I am not assuming McDonald's wants a platform replacement. My working thesis is that the credible openings, if any, are narrower: second-source DMB, approved hardware optionality, or a camera-based drive-thru timing capability. Where is that right, and where is it naive?”

Follow with process questions:

- Who would own each evaluation path?
- What evidence, sponsorship, certification, incumbent alignment, and franchisee acceptance would be required?
- Does the CosMc's work give Genius legitimate credibility, or is it viewed as isolated innovation work?
- What would make Jeff advise Todd to stop pursuing a particular path?

### 5. Map contacts before asking for access

The goal is a role map first, names second, and introductions only if Jeff volunteers or recommends them.

- Who owns second-source DMB strategy and supplier risk?
- Who owns restaurant hardware standards, certification, and deployment approval?
- Who owns drive-thru timing and speed-of-service measurement?
- Who owns U.S. technology execution versus global restaurant architecture?
- Who at McDonald's can credibly validate what Genius delivered at CosMc's?
- Who inside Global Payments/Genius owns the CosMc's relationship and proof package?
- Is Josh Wesolowski still connected to the relevant work, and what is the appropriate path to him?
- Are Amanda Thomas or Mike Skog relevant early validators, or would Jeff point elsewhere?
- Where do supplier management, Speedee Labs, restaurant operations, and owner/operators enter the decision?

Suggested wording:

> “I am trying to map ownership before I approach anyone. For the capabilities McDonald's saw Genius operate at CosMc's—especially DMB, hardware, and drive-thru timing—who owns the problem, who validates the technology, and who controls the supplier path?”

## Current hypothesis—hold lightly

- Core NEWPOS replacement is not a credible opening.
- McDonald's has substantial investment in its own platform and Edge direction.
- Second-source DMB is the most credible wedge if supplier-concentration or rollout pain is real.
- Hardware optionality could matter if NCR/PAR performance issues are material.

Treat vendor-performance claims as hypotheses until Jeff validates them. Do not name confidential sources or overstate Coates, NCR, PAR, HP, MAPS, QSRSoft, Harri, or Stratacache conclusions.
