## Member-fit criteria
- Worked at PAR Technology at any point.

## Why this Circle exists
The PAR alumni network is one of Todd's most concentrated relationship clusters — 120+ baseline connections currently or formerly at PAR. Many of his inner-tier RCs came up together at PAR and share the institutional shorthand, deal context, and mutual trust that produced. Treating them as a Circle makes that shared substrate visible: when one of them moves companies, the news matters to the others; when a deal involves PAR, the cohort knows the dynamics; when an introduction is being considered, this Circle is the natural look-up table for warm-path proximity.
