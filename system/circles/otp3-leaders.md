## Why this Circle exists
This cohort already operates daily without system intervention — they don't need re-engagement, prompting, or activation. The Circle's job is the opposite: make sure the system *recognizes* this as a singular relationship structure rather than treating its members as four independent inner-tier RCs. Tagging keeps cohort-relevant context visible in `today.md` (e.g. cohort-shared news, awards, transitions) and prevents the system from suggesting actions that misread the cadence (no nudge-to-reconnect for a relationship that touches daily).

It also marks the institutional history: McDonald's franchise side → OTP Level 3 program leaders → presented at the first OTP3 conferences → award winners → split paths (Todd to vendor first, Bruce to corporate, Dave to vendor leadership, John to PAR via Todd's hire).
