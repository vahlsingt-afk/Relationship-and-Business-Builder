## Account-fit criteria
- Domain: hospitality / restaurant tech, restaurant operators with internal tech leadership, or adjacent (food-service SaaS, hospitality CX, multi-location ops). For restaurant-tech vendors, use `system/restaurant_tech_watchlist.md` as the category definition: POS, back office, payments, loyalty/CRM, digital menu boards, online ordering, restaurant AI, POS hardware, drive-thru camera/communication devices, KDS/kitchen ops, guest experience, and enterprise integration layers.
- Stage: Series B through public for tech vendors; established multi-location operators for restaurants.
- Geography: open, U.S. preference.
- Working-style: leadership team that values outside-in perspective, has actual decision-making latitude, and isn't pre-committed to a specific firm or hire.

## Two paths these accounts can produce
- **Consulting engagement** — short-form (audit, strategy sprint) or longer-form (fractional, embedded).
- **Full-time role** — only if the right one. Tracking here lets you spot the right one when it appears, rather than reacting to inbound noise.

## How this Circle progresses
1. You add a target account.
2. The system queries `baseline_index.json` for any connection at that company and proposes the best inroad.
3. If no direct connection, the system identifies the best 2nd-degree path through `intro_brokers.md`.
4. Status: `target` → `inroad-identified` → `conversation-opened` → `proposal-or-interview` → `won` / `lost` / `parked`.
