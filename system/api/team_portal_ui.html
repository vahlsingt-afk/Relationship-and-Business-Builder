What this is

The RBB Ecosystem Lookup Tool is a shared, searchable view into restaurant brand and technology-vendor intelligence: company facts, leadership, tech stack, and competitive positioning. It's read-first — browse anything on file, and optionally add tech-stack entries or suggest corrections for review.

How to use it

  1. Pick Brands or Vendors above, then search by name -- or use Partner Finder to search by product, capability, or a problem to solve (e.g. "labor scheduling", "digital menu boards").
  2. Open a result to see its profile: identity, leadership, recent signals, and technology environment.
  3. Know something that's missing or wrong? Use the "Suggest a correction" form on that page — it's queued for review, never applied live.
  4. On a brand page, you can also log a tech-stack entry directly (vendor, category, deployment stage).
