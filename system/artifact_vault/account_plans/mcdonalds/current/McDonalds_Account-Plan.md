## Account Strategy
Use the existing McDonald's strategic plan as the governing pursuit thesis. Do not lead with a broad platform or core-POS replacement. Pursue three independently approvable, operational wedges in priority order: (1) second-source drive-thru digital menu boards to reduce supplier concentration and execution risk, (2) approved POS-hardware optionality where incumbent dissatisfaction can be validated, and (3) camera-based drive-thru timing as a contained, measurable alternative to loop detectors. Use CosMc's only as evidence that Global Payments/Genius has operated inside the McDonald's ecosystem, not as proof that the CosMc's stack belongs in the core estate. Pressure-test ownership, pain, approval requirements, economics, and proof standards with trusted McDonald's and Global Payments contacts before broad outreach. Build the proof package around uptime, support, deployment, hardware certification, privacy/security, and a side-by-side timer pilot. Use the active McDonald's U.S. Operations micrograph—14,061 stores and 1,338 operator entities mapped—to understand franchise structure, field-office and co-op topology, identify representative operators, and plan any pilot or phased deployment; treat it as an internal targeting and deployment-planning asset rather than customer-stated demand.
