## Category Battle Cards

### Payments

**Status:** source_backed (confidence: 75%)

**RM posture:** Treat Shift4 as a broadening payments-platform competitor, not merely a processor. Its acquisition strategy is filling payment-method gaps so merchants have fewer reasons to use another provider.

**Listen for:**
- Shift4 using DCC, ACH, and account-to-account payments to argue merchants no longer need another payment provider.

**Discovery questions:**
- Which restaurant transaction types materially require account-to-account payments today, and what adoption or economics have been demonstrated beyond private-dining and event deposits?

**When to bring Todd in:** Bring Todd in when Shift4 uses A2A, ACH, DCC, or high-ticket restaurant-payment use cases to support a consolidated-payments pitch.
