# RB 9.0 Strategic Direction — Chief of Staff Platform

**Date:** 2026-05-24  
**Status:** foundational product direction  
**Purpose:** preserve the strategic architecture guidance for RB's evolution from relationship-memory tooling into a true Chief of Staff platform.

## Product North Star

RB is not a news feed, CRM wrapper, task manager, or disconnected productivity assistant. RB should behave like a trusted strategic operator sitting beside the executive.

The platform value is:

- less noise;
- better timing;
- higher-quality decisions;
- preserved relationships;
- strategic clarity;
- operational awareness;
- executive focus.

The product doctrine is judgment over noise.

RB also follows the restaurant reality intelligence model in
`system/RB_9_RESTAURANT_REALITY_INTELLIGENCE_MODEL.md`: decision quality over
worldview reinforcement, delta-first intelligence, operational truth detection,
pain mapping, macro pressure stacking, unit economics, and franchisor/franchisee
alignment gaps.

## Chief Of Staff Behavior

RB should continuously answer:

- What matters right now?
- What does not matter yet?
- Who requires attention?
- What should wait?
- What risks are emerging?
- What opportunities are developing?
- What should the user not spend time on?

Target behavior:

- strategic prioritization;
- relationship intelligence;
- contextual judgment;
- proactive recommendations;
- operational awareness;
- executive filtering;
- timing sensitivity;
- trust preservation.

## Daily Brief Vision

The daily brief is not a writing task. It is the visible trust surface of RB's operational memory.

It should connect:

```text
industry news + user relationships + strategic goals + operational signals
```

Example: "Company X selected Vendor Y" should trigger:

- who in the user's network is connected;
- who to contact;
- possible implications;
- competitive positioning;
- suggested outreach;
- timing guidance.

Required daily brief posture:

- source verification before interpretation;
- explicit evidence that supports and challenges active theses;
- delta-first intelligence before narrative synthesis;
- macroeconomic pressure stacking against restaurant operations and tech buying;
- pain mapping and operational truth detection;
- franchisor/franchisee alignment-gap analysis when a signal involves brands, franchisees, rollout, or standards;
- prioritized executive guidance;
- relationship-driven recommendations;
- operational risk visibility;
- strategic opportunity surfacing;
- macroeconomic context;
- industry intelligence tied to the user's actual network and goals.

## Passive RI Persistence

Passive relationship intelligence must become automatic.

Every meaningful interaction should automatically update or propose updates to:

- relationship strength;
- trust indicators;
- last touch;
- sentiment;
- opportunity signals;
- strategic relevance;
- follow-up suggestions;
- timeline continuity.

Inputs should include:

- email;
- calendar;
- meeting transcripts;
- LinkedIn activity;
- text/call metadata;
- uploaded documents;
- CRM updates;
- notes;
- interaction frequency.

Manual "capture RI" should become an exception path, not the normal workflow.

## Canonical Trust Language

RB must speak with certainty only when certainty is earned.

Use when completed:

- "Relationship intelligence detected and recorded."
- "Trust score updated."
- "Opportunity surfaced."
- "Follow-up loop created."

Avoid when the system actually completed the action:

- "should be marked"
- "could be updated"

Every response should distinguish:

- action completed;
- action inferred;
- action suggested;
- action blocked;
- action unavailable.

## User-Agnostic Architecture

RB must become user-agnostic, industry-agnostic, configurable, and profile-driven.

Core engine:

- judgment;
- prioritization;
- orchestration;
- relationship intelligence;
- workflow management;
- source/provenance verification.

User layer:

- industry context;
- communication style;
- strategic goals;
- company data;
- terminology;
- integrations;
- preferences.

This prevents Todd-specific assumptions from hardening into the platform core.

## Local Persistence And User Ownership

Long-term architecture should support:

- downloadable user profiles;
- portable configuration/state;
- local persistence options;
- user-controlled storage;
- self-hosted or hybrid deployment paths.

Users should own:

- their relationship graph;
- their interaction history;
- their operational memory;
- their strategic context.

RB should not become a black-box SaaS dependency.

## Security Foundation

Security cannot be retrofitted later.

Future architecture should assume:

- encryption-ready storage;
- local-first options;
- access controls;
- authentication;
- permission boundaries;
- secure ingestion pipelines;
- source/mutation auditability.

This is mandatory if RB continuously ingests email, transcripts, calls, LinkedIn, CRM, calendar, and documents.

RB's security doctrine is now:

- memory by judgment, not memory by volume;
- raw source data separated from derived intelligence;
- least-privilege OAuth and connector scopes;
- retention classes for raw, normalized, derived, durable, audit, and forgettable data;
- prompt-injection resistance for all external artifacts;
- explicit action gates before mutating email, files, Drive, CRM, calendar, profile, or relationship state;
- audit logs for what RB accessed, persisted, rejected, changed, and why.

## Automation And Friction Reduction

Target experience:

```text
Configure once, benefit continuously.
```

Example passive transcript flow:

1. AI meeting transcript is exported into a monitored folder.
2. RB detects the transcript.
3. RB ingests the transcript.
4. RB extracts relationships, commitments, tasks, opportunities, trust signals, and strategic themes.
5. RB updates relationship state or proposes review-first mutations.
6. RB surfaces only actionable outcomes.

No manual processing workflow should be required for routine ingestion.

The strategic goal is continuous passive intelligence gathering with executive-level synthesis.
