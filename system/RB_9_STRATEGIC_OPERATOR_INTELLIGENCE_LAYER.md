# RB 9.0 Feature Brief — Strategic Operator Intelligence Layer

## Objective

Upgrade the Daily Brief from industry-news aggregation into operator-centric strategic intelligence.

RB must identify and prioritize large franchise operators, multi-brand operators, consolidators, PE-backed restaurant operators, regional scale operators, and high-influence franchisee groups as strategic relationship entities.

This is a Chief-of-Staff intelligence function, not a news summary feature.

## Core Principle

RB should not merely report operator events. It must interpret operator events through the lens of:

- influence
- relationship value
- operational pressure
- ecosystem impact
- likely future opportunity

The system must answer:

- why this matters strategically
- who matters now
- which operators are becoming more influential
- what operational pressure this creates
- where relationships should be built intentionally
- which entities should enter persistent strategic watchlists

## Target Entity Types

- Strategic franchise operators
- Multi-brand franchise groups
- PE-backed restaurant operators
- Large regional operators
- High-influence franchisee groups
- Operator holding companies

Examples include Flynn Group, Carrols Restaurant Group, Sailormen, Ghai Management, Thrive Restaurant Group, Sizzling Platter, KBP Foods, MuY Companies, and NPC successor entities.

## Strategic Operator Movements

The Daily Brief must include a `Strategic Operator Movements` layer when source-backed operator events exist.

This section is not chronological news. It is interpreted intelligence.

Track acquisitions, divestitures, bankruptcies, restructures, executive moves, concept/geographic expansion, franchise transfers, PE activity, technology standardization signals, operational scaling indicators, labor pressure, unit growth velocity, vendor transitions, and cross-brand operational behavior.

## Interpretation Engine

For each significant operator event, RB should infer operational complexity increase, standardization pressure, likely technology reassessment, scaling strain, integration risk, labor coordination pressure, reporting visibility needs, vendor consolidation likelihood, and future buying-window probability.

These inferences must be labeled and confidence-scored. They should never be presented as observed facts unless the source directly says them.

## Relationship Intelligence Requirements

For each significant operator event, RB should map relationship proximity, identify known network overlap, identify mutual connections, identify existing vendor relationships, determine strategic importance score, determine ecosystem influence score, determine operational pressure score, determine future opportunity score, and determine follow-up priority.

## Persistent Watchlists

Create and maintain persistent watchlists for:

- Strategic Operators
- Multi-Brand Operators
- Expansion Leaders
- High-Influence Franchisees
- PE-Backed Groups

Track expansion velocity, acquisition frequency, leadership changes, operational stress signals, technology stack changes, relationship proximity, and buying-window probability over time.

## Output Contract

Daily Brief item format:

```text
Strategic Operator Movement
System-detected

Entity:
Ghai Management acquired 44 Taco Bell locations.

Why it matters:
- Increased operational scale.
- Likely standardization pressure.
- Increased complexity around labor, inventory, and reporting.
- Multi-brand operators influence peer operator buying behavior.

Relationship implications:
- Strategic relationship target.
- High downstream ecosystem influence.
- Potential technology evaluation window after integration period.

Recommended actions:
- Monitor leadership changes.
- Map mutual connections.
- Track vendor ecosystem.
- Watch for operational hiring or implementation activity.

Confidence:
High
```

## Governance

The system must never merely summarize articles.

RB must interpret, prioritize, contextualize, operationalize, and connect to relationship strategy.

Signals should persist into relationship scoring, watchlists, future brief prioritization, opportunity detection, network mapping, and strategic recommendations.

## Success Criteria

The daily brief should surface who is becoming strategically important, why they matter operationally, how they influence the ecosystem, where relationship investment should occur, and what future opportunities may emerge.

The system should feel like an operator-savvy strategic Chief of Staff, not a restaurant industry newsletter.
