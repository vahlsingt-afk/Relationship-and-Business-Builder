# Del Taco — Executive Summary

Del Taco is now a Yadav Enterprises-owned, predominantly franchised Mexican QSR entering a post-acquisition transformation. Yadav acquired the brand from Jack in the Box in late 2025; the announced purchase price was $115 million, and Jack in the Box later reported $119 million after customary adjustments. At fiscal year-end September 28, 2025, Del Taco had 576 restaurants—132 company-operated and 444 franchised—and generated $918.8 million in systemwide sales. That inherited baseline was under pressure: FY2025 system same-store sales declined 3.7%, 14 restaurants opened, 32 closed, and reported restaurant-level margin was 11.1%.

Yadav's January 2026 leadership reset signals an active transformation agenda. Confirmed leaders include Anil Yadav, CEO of Yadav Enterprises; Ulyses Camacho, Del Taco's newly appointed Chief Transformation Officer; Noah Chillingworth, Chief Marketing Officer; and Ellen Sasada, Vice President of Supply Chain, Food Safety, and Quality Assurance. Yadav says its broader portfolio includes more than 300 franchise restaurants and positions itself as the largest franchisee of Jack in the Box, Denny's, and TGI Fridays, as well as franchisor of Taco Cabana and Nick the Greek.

The technology picture contains both opportunity and a major qualification requirement. Qu announced in 2024 that Jack in the Box and Del Taco would modernize on its unified commerce platform across POS, drive-thru, kiosk, kitchen, online ordering, and fulfillment. Del Taco also has historical NCR/Radiant Aloha and MenuLink evidence, a publicly announced Presto drive-thru voice-AI deployment, an RB-carried Olo loyalty relationship at medium confidence, and a high-confidence Worldpay payments relationship. The post-acquisition status of each system—especially Qu—must be validated before positioning Genius.

Todd's September 2026 chat with Michael Schwartz adds timely internal account intelligence. Michael characterized Del Taco as an upcoming opportunity and emphasized strong alignment with Worldpay enterprise stakeholders. Todd reported that Worldpay was in the middle of the contract renewal and that this was a good moment to engage. Todd planned a discussion with Maggie and proposed a hardware 201 plus P&L walkthrough for the Genius and Worldpay teams. Michael identified Dean Kimball at Yadav Enterprises as highly influential and a long-standing customer from the Xpient/IRIS era, and described Del Taco as a possible entry point into the broader Yadav portfolio. These are valuable internal signals, not independently verified public facts; Dean's current role and decision authority must be confirmed.

The recommended posture is a relationship-led, discovery-first wedge—not a generic replacement pitch. First, use Maggie and the Worldpay team to document the exact renewal scope, commercial dates, account ownership, and access path. Second, run the hardware/P&L session to understand device lifecycle, rollout economics, support obligations, and where Genius can create measurable value. Third, validate Qu's contract and deployment status by store cohort. The most credible initial motion may be hardware, payments-linked economics, integration, DMB, or another modular coexistence play. A broader POS pursuit should follow only if the inherited stack, Yadav's priorities, and decision process support it.

## Key Discovery Questions

### Business and Operating Priorities
- What are current leadership's top strategic priorities?
- What operating metrics (comps, traffic, margin) are under the most pressure right now?
- Is there an active turnaround, growth push, or ownership/leadership change underway?
- What is driving any current technology or vendor evaluation, if one exists?

### Loyalty and Digital
- What loyalty platform is currently in use, if any?
- What percentage of transactions come through loyalty or digital channels?
- What are the current app, online-ordering, and delivery platforms?
- Who owns digital engagement and customer data internally?

### Payments
- Who processes in-store and digital payments today?
- Is there a single payments gateway/acquirer, or a fragmented setup across channels?
- When do the current payments agreements renew?
- Are there known pain points (fees, chargebacks, fraud, reliability)?

### Restaurant Technology
- What POS system is currently deployed?
- What hardware is installed, and is there a known refresh or support deadline?
- What systems handle KDS, back office, inventory, and labor management?
- Are there known integration or data-quality issues across systems?

### Decision Process
- Who owns technology strategy and vendor decisions?
- Who owns loyalty, payments, and digital commerce internally?
- Is there an existing Global Payments/Genius relationship or contact?
- What are the next known renewal, budgeting, or vendor-review dates?
