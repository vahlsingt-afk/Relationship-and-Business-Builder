## Engagement Rules (hard)

These are rules, not preferences. RB should apply them when classifying inbound
asks and when surfacing recommendations.

**Will not be positioned as:**
- Free strategy resource
- Intro broker with no compensation or conviction
- Commission-only sales channel

**Acceptable engagement models:**
- Paid Strategic Engagement (paid up front)
- Hybrid BD Execution (retainer + success — only with strong product fit and conviction)
- Pure Referral (rare, controlled, only with high conviction in product, fit, and deployment capability)

**Core rule:** No unpaid thinking. No introductions without conviction. No
advisory without payment. Relationship capital is not free inventory.

## Intro Boundaries

- Will only make introductions when Todd believes both parties will benefit
- Will not do cold blasts on behalf of others
- Intro requests from outside the inner circle require demonstrated product/fit conviction before Todd acts

## Conflict Rules

- No simultaneous competing vendor advisory relationships without explicit disclosure
- No introductions into clients where Todd has a paid engagement without client consent
- BridgePoint Ops engagements are confidential by default — do not surface client names in public content

## Privacy Rules

- Relationship intelligence stays local — do not surface sensitive personal information in any output intended for sharing
- Do not infer sensitive facts about contacts without direct evidence
- Do not propose mutations that would expose private relationship context

## Employer / Client Constraints

- Current BridgePoint Ops clients are confidential
- PAR Technology and NomadGo relationships are professional; past employment context may be used for network mapping but not for competitive intelligence sharing

## Things That Should Trigger a Watch Flag (not an action flag)

- Inbound requests that read as "free advice" or "pick your brain"
- Intro asks from people outside Todd's inner network
- Vendor outreach requesting case studies or customer intros without engagement discussion first
- Any request where Todd would be giving disproportionate value relative to compensation received
