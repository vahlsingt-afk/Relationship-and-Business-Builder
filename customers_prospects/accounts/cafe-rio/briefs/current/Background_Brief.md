## Executive Summary
Café Rio Fresh Modern Mexican is a Salt Lake City-based fast-casual Mexican chain with approximately 150-160 company-owned restaurants. The company is majority-owned by private equity firm Freeman Spogli and appointed Mike Burns as CEO in June 2026.

Café Rio is currently undertaking an operational and brand reset. Management has publicly acknowledged slower service, inconsistent restaurant cleanliness, unpopular menu changes and declining comparable sales. The immediate focus is restoring food quality, improving execution and rebuilding guest frequency before accelerating new-unit growth around 2028.

Café Rio is a current or former Punchh loyalty customer, and management says loyalty members account for upward of 50% of transactions. That makes loyalty and guest engagement especially important to the turnaround. Beyond Punchh and an older reference to Aloha POS, research has uncovered almost no reliable current information about Café Rio's technology stack.

The account should be treated as a strong discovery target rather than a qualified sales pursuit.

## Current Business Situation
Mike Burns became CEO in June 2026 after leading Latitude Food Group, the parent organization of &pizza and Tijuana Flats.

Burns has publicly described several issues facing the company: Café Rio had "lost its way" operationally; changes to established menu items frustrated longtime customers; service became slower; restaurant cleanliness declined; same-store sales were down approximately 2.3% during the first part of 2026; heavy spending on third-party delivery and discounting generated transactions that were not necessarily profitable; the brand needs to restore its core experience before resuming aggressive expansion.

Immediate priorities: (1) restore original recipes and food quality; (2) improve restaurant speed, friendliness and cleanliness; (3) rebuild guest trust and visit frequency; (4) reduce dependence on indiscriminate discounting; (5) return to positive comparable sales; (6) prepare the operating platform for renewed growth beginning around 2028.

This turnaround is the most important context for any conversation with Café Rio.

## Why Loyalty Matters
Café Rio's loyalty population appears to represent roughly half of its transactions. Management wants to use that audience to drive frequency and engagement without relying on constant discounts. Key questions: is Punchh still the active platform; who owns loyalty and guest engagement under the new leadership team; how effectively can Café Rio segment and activate its customers; can it measure incremental visits without over-discounting; are POS, ordering, payments and loyalty data properly connected; what is the current contract/renewal timeline; is there an optimization, expansion or competitive-review event?

## Recommended Engagement Strategy
Start with account validation and operational discovery rather than a broad product presentation. Credible framing: 'Café Rio is working to restore the guest experience, improve restaurant execution and prepare for its next growth phase. We would like to understand where technology, payments and customer data are helping -- or creating friction -- in that effort.' Initial objectives: (1) a verified leadership and stakeholder map; (2) a current technology-stack map; (3) a current loyalty and payments assessment; (4) a contract and renewal calendar; (5) the top two or three operating problems affecting the turnaround; (6) one specific, sponsor-backed opportunity worth advancing.

## Bottom Line
Café Rio is a centralized, PE-backed restaurant brand with a new CEO, visible operating challenges and a strategically important loyalty population. Its technology environment remains unusually opaque.

The account is worth pursuing because the turnaround creates potential need across loyalty, payments, restaurant execution and growth readiness. It is not yet qualified because we still lack a confirmed sponsor, current stack, internal owner, commercial timing and a defined buying event.

Current recommendation: validate the relationship and stack, identify the operational trigger, and then determine whether Café Rio should advance from a research target into an active opportunity.
