## Bottom line

Global Payments/Genius should not approach McDonald's as a broad platform-replacement opportunity. McDonald's has heavy investment in its own restaurant platform, Google Cloud/Edge direction, loyalty/customer engagement, and embedded operating systems. The realistic path is a narrow, operational wedge that helps McDonald's reduce supplier risk and improve U.S. execution without challenging its core strategy.

The three credible approval paths are:

1. **Second-source drive-thru digital menu boards (DMB).**
2. **POS hardware / approved hardware optionality.**
3. **Camera-based drive-thru timer / speed-of-service measurement.** This capability operated in the CosMc's stack and can be evaluated against the loop-detector systems used across much of the McDonald's estate without replacing NEWPOS or the DMB platform.

CosMc's matters because it proves Global Payments/Genius is not unknown to McDonald's. It should be used as credibility proof, not as a claim that the CosMc's stack should move into the core McDonald's estate.

The latest processed JPR conversation (`cap-3654fabbc0e36a68`, 2026-08-03) reinforces the commercial logic: drive-thru timers are a modular wedge when the customer is committed to another POS. The goal is to land a useful component, establish Global Payments/Genius as a trusted operating partner, and earn preferred-vendor position for future expansion—not to make timer approval contingent on a full-platform decision.

## Strategic thesis

McDonald's is signaling a need for practical execution improvement, not a desire to rip out the core platform. The U.S. business is under execution pressure, franchisee adoption is a visible issue, and leadership is changing with Skye Anderson moving into the McDonald's USA president role. That context strengthens a supplier-risk and field-reliability conversation.

The right message:

> Global Payments/Genius can help McDonald's add optionality and resilience in three specific restaurant-technology lanes: second-source DMB, approved POS hardware, and camera-based drive-thru timing. Each can be evaluated independently while respecting the current McDonald's platform, Edge investment, loyalty ownership, back-office systems, workforce systems, and franchise operating reality.

## What not to pitch

Do not pitch McDonald's on:

- Core POS / NEWPOS replacement.
- Loyalty or customer engagement replacement.
- Back office replacement.
- Workforce management replacement.
- Broad CosMc's platform transfer.

Those lanes are either McDonald's-owned or incumbent-managed. Treat them as integration boundaries, not opportunity wedges. Todd field assessment: QSRSoft is entrenched in domestic U.S. McDonald's back office for cash, inventory, and reporting.

## Proof required before outreach expands

Global Payments/Genius should gather:

- Exact CosMc's contracted scope and components.
- DMB architecture and whether media-player-free deployment is viable for McDonald's.
- Camera-timer production scope and performance: detection/timing accuracy, missed and false detections, lane attribution, day/night and weather performance, uptime, calibration, privacy/security, data retention, installation, maintenance, and support.
- A side-by-side camera-versus-loop pilot design with pre-agreed accuracy, availability, operating, and economic thresholds.
- Uptime, support, incident, recovery, and deployment evidence.
- Field-service and replacement model.
- Hardware certification path.
- Security and integration requirements.
- Named McDonald's owners for DMB, hardware, and drive-thru timing standards/evaluation.
- Clear boundary language around loyalty, back office, and workforce management.
- A one-page Worldpay relationship-manager enablement brief: timer value story, loop-detector qualification questions, ideal pilot profile, disqualifiers, proof required, and Genius-specialist handoff.
