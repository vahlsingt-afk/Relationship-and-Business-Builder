## Executive Summary
Five Guys is a privately held, family-controlled fast-casual system approaching 2,000 restaurants in 30 countries. Public sources show a mature digital and operating stack: PAR Brink for the core U.S. POS and reporting environment, Olo for ordering and guest-data activation, Crunchtime for inventory and labor, and automated testing around Brink upgrades. Worldpay intelligence also identifies NCR in EMEA, Verifone devices, and LineTen, but scope and geography still require validation.

The account is #2 in Worldpay's current portfolio, P1 / Tier 1, score 82, but customer-directed outreach is now on explicit hold. On September 4, MJ Worsham wrote that Five Guys is already working with Worldpay as a long-term customer, has no interest in evaluating other Global Payments solutions at this time, and will work through Avery Churchwell and Melissa Douglas if that changes. Melissa escalated the uncoordinated outreach internally as “not a great look.”

There is no confirmed RFI, RFP, budget, or replacement mandate, and no verified PAR failure. Global architecture, support gaps, payments/device harmonization, and contained expansion remain internal research hypotheses—not active sales motions. A capped transaction-metered technology-funding concept is under internal exploration only; it is not approved or customer-presented.

Immediate priorities: stop proactive Genius outreach, cancel any unconfirmed September 15–16 meeting pursuit, align one contact policy across the internal team, and let Avery or Melissa decide whether and when the account can be reopened. Estate mapping and commercial analysis may continue internally.

## Key Discovery Questions

### Business and Operating Priorities
- What are current leadership's top strategic priorities?
- What operating metrics (comps, traffic, margin) are under the most pressure right now?
- Is there an active turnaround, growth push, or ownership/leadership change underway?
- What is driving any current technology or vendor evaluation, if one exists?

### Loyalty and Digital
- What loyalty platform is currently in use, if any?
- What percentage of transactions come through loyalty or digital channels?
- What are the current app, online-ordering, and delivery platforms?
- Who owns digital engagement and customer data internally?

### Payments
- Who processes in-store and digital payments today?
- Is there a single payments gateway/acquirer, or a fragmented setup across channels?
- When do the current payments agreements renew?
- Are there known pain points (fees, chargebacks, fraud, reliability)?

### Restaurant Technology
- What POS system is currently deployed?
- What hardware is installed, and is there a known refresh or support deadline?
- What systems handle KDS, back office, inventory, and labor management?
- Are there known integration or data-quality issues across systems?

### Decision Process
- Who owns technology strategy and vendor decisions?
- Who owns loyalty, payments, and digital commerce internally?
- Is there an existing Global Payments/Genius relationship or contact?
- What are the next known renewal, budgeting, or vendor-review dates?
